Week 4: Programming

For this weeks electronic project I wanted to work on my previous kinteic sculpture and this time I wanted to adjust its wing flapping speed.

Firstly, I remade the entire system. I first cut a larger box and made the cam as a of centered circle to since the nautilus shell-like cam was not working to to friction. So I made it a circle and also wrapped the cam with vinyl to reduce friction. Also I added cylinders at the bottom of the sticks so they won't get stuck to the cam and try to rotate instead of an up and down motion. I used a thicker wood to as a shaft and used cardboard bearings for extra stability. Also I reguled the connections of the sticks, so it will be a stronger structure. Moreover, I added a second cardboard plane so it would hold the sticks under the wings at 2 points for better stability. Even though it works with hand, it does not works with the esp32 because the motor wasn't powerful enough and it couldn't rotated the total weight. In the future I might try using it with a powerful motor.
