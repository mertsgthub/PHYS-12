Week 3: Fabrication

I love aviation, so I like to work on projects that includes flight or aerodynamic. For that reason I decided to work on a kinetic sculpture of a bird that flaps its wings. For the sculpture itself I used multiple layers of cardboard that I designed from Onshape and cut from laser cutter. Than I created a box and integrated a circuit with cam shafft to move the wings up and down. My first trial was unsuccessful because sticks didn't move just up and down. So this week I am planning to resolve this issue.
