Final Project Updates

For the final project I chose my first idea: The seed dropper. I am planning to do its body from 3D print and attachment parts mostly from laser cutter. I might use cnc to create the storge of the dropper out of wood instead of using 3D printer. For the mechanism itsef I am thinking to use laser cutted beams, servo motors(maybe dc as well), esp32, and if I can find the resources I am planning to integrate it to a remote control via receiver, since it is a mechanism specially designed to be used in drones and rc planes, so it would be more realistic and functional to control it with a remote controller. I am planning to design a rotating mechanism like ones in paintball to dispense it uniformly. I think the hardest part will be the figuring out how to dispense it with consistency and without jamming. Moreover, I need to make it lightweight as possible.
