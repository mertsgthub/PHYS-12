Week 7: Outputs

For my MVP I did a prototype with 2 input and 4 output devices. Also, I used ESP-now to wirelessly connect 2 esp32's. I used a wireless control because this seed dropper is intended to be used in drones or rc planes.

Firstly, I designed the parts as bpth 2D and 3D. I cut the flat parts from laser cutter and printed the storage unit from 3D printer.
